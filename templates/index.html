Capabilities

Engineered for the Ultimate Comic Experience

Designed from the ground up for seamless cloud reading with zero lag, instant page flips, and intuitive controls.

☁️

Google Drive Integration

Authenticate with Google OAuth 2.0 and stream comics directly from your personal Drive. No manual file transferring or cloud syncing apps required.

📦

Multi-Format Engine

Built-in parser for standard .cbz archives, 7-Zip decompression for .cbr archives, and native rasterization for .pdf comic books.

⚡

Smart Predictive Preloader

Intelligent 2-phase caching engine preloads upcoming and previous pages with concurrency pooling, guaranteeing instantaneous slide transitions.

🔍

Smooth Zoom & Pan Engine

Deep zoom up to 300% with native smooth scrolling, trackpad pinch gestures, mouse drag-to-pan, and continuous joystick panning.

📺

Smart TV & Fire TV Mode

Auto-detects Fire TV and WebOS to enable safe overscan margins, spatial D-pad navigation, remote media keys (⏪ ⏩ ⏯️), and focus indicators.

🎮

Universal Gamepad Support

Plug in Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock / DualSense), or generic controllers to navigate, zoom with analog triggers/thumbsticks, and turn pages.
